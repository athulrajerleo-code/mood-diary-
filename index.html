    Calming skills: back to my window of tolerance

    When I feel too much

    Hyperarousal: racing heart, panic, anger, restless, can’t switch off

    • Slow breathing: in for 4, out for 6–8, for 2–3 minutes
    • Box breathing: in 4, hold 4, out 4, hold 4
    • 5-4-3-2-1 grounding: name 5 things you see, 4 you hear, 3 you feel, 2 you smell, 1 you taste
    • Muscle relaxation: tense each muscle group for 5 seconds, then release
    • Butterfly hug: cross your arms and tap your shoulders slowly, left and right

    When I feel too little

    Hypoarousal: numb, empty, foggy, heavy, shut down

    • Move: stand up, stretch, walk briskly or shake out your arms
    • Orient: look around the room and name 5 colours aloud
    • Energising breath: a quick sniff in through the nose, a long sigh out
    • Engage the senses: upbeat music, a strong scent, a textured object
    • Connect: call or message someone you feel safe with
