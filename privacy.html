Privacy policy

My Mood & Stress Diary · Last updated 16 October 2026

What the app collects

Nothing. The app has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no server that receives your data.

Where your entries are kept

Your moods, sleep hours, stressors and notes are stored only on your own phone, inside the app's storage. They never leave the device unless you choose to share them.

Sharing

When you tap Share, Copy or Save, the app creates a summary or backup file and hands it to the app you pick, such as WhatsApp, email or Google Drive. What happens to it after that is governed by that app.

Deleting your data

Uninstalling the app, or clearing its storage in your phone's settings, permanently deletes every entry. Keep a backup if you want to keep your history.

Not a medical service

This diary helps you notice patterns and talk about them with your doctor. It does not diagnose or treat any condition. If you are in crisis, call Tele MANAS on 14416 or go to the nearest hospital.
